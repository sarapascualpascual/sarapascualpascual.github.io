About

Senior Aerospace Engineer with 8+ years of experience in safety-critical environments, combining a strong engineering background with service evolution, operational decision-making and stakeholder coordination.

At ESSP, I work on the evolution of EGNOS, Europe’s Satellite-Based Augmentation System. I am responsible for contingency management activities, assessing their operational impact, determining how they affect service provision and defining the appropriate service response and applicability.

I also develop and issue service and technical communications related to contingencies and operational events, while coordinating with technical, operational and external stakeholders and contributing to service roadmapping, change implementation and continuous improvement.

My previous experience includes Verification and Validation for Airbus commercial aircraft, R&D of Airborne Wind Energy Systems, engineering modelling and simulation, and software product consulting.

My multidisciplinary background enables me to understand complex technical environments while focusing on service evolution, operational needs, stakeholder coordination and long-term strategy.
